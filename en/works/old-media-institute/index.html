Overview

旧介质研究所 (Old Media Institute) is an adventure game from a college design team centred on four game-design students at Communication University of China. Players control anthropomorphic artefacts, fight post-apocalyptic monsters and gradually discover the truth about the world.

As narrative designer I created several characters drawn from cultural heritage, giving each a personality and a backstory; shaped the game’s worldview; and wrote part of the character dialogue for the prologue.
