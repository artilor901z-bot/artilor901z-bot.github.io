TRACK 01 · 2024–25

TRACK 01

Myth of Lumi

Role — Game designer — card abilities, throw feel (juice), onboarding UI

  • Systems & gameplay

Duel rival magicians by throwing cards onto a 3D table. Each card holds a Lumi, a creature that fights for turf where it lands. I designed the card abilities, the feel of the throw and the in-game onboarding.

HIGHLIGHTS

  • ◆Shipped free on Steam in August 2025 — Very Positive user reviews
  • ◆Gold at the 2025 Tencent Game Creation Contest; IGF 2026 Best Student Game honorable mention
  • ◆One designer on a ~50-person USC Games team

§02

Overview

Myth of Lumi is a 3D physics-based turf-war game: you duel rival magicians in card-throwing showdowns, recruiting Lumis — mystical creatures bound inside your playing cards — to fight for you and carve your path to fame and fortune.
